Search the whole `@` menu with one query. Names with spaces are now searchable, so `@my report.txt` finds the file it names, and past chats are found by title in the list instead of only behind **Past chats**. Files, chats, and entries such as **Terminal**, **Git changes**, and **Browse files...** are ranked together by how well they fit the query, rather than listed in fixed groups.
