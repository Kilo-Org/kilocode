Fade list row titles and descriptions into the row background where they do not fit, instead of ending them in an ellipsis. Applies to every list built on the shared row renderer, including the Agent Manager worktree list, the session list, and the settings lists. Turn it off with the `kilo.list.fade` registry key to leave the text cut at the row edge instead.
