# Data Exports

The [Data exports](https://app.kilo.ai/data-exports) page on the Kilo dashboard lets you download an archive of the data stored with your account or with one of your organizations.

## Personal Exports

A personal export covers your own account data. It includes your App Builder project titles and the prompt prefixes recorded with your usage history.

1. Open the [Data exports](https://app.kilo.ai/data-exports) page
2. Request a new export — large accounts can take a while to generate
3. Kilo emails you when the export is ready

Downloads expire 24 hours after the ready email. If an export has expired, request a new one. You can request one new export per hour.

### Download Confirmation Codes

Because an export is a single archive of everything stored with your account, downloading one takes an extra verification step beyond being signed in:

1. Click **Download** on a ready export
2. Kilo emails a 6-digit confirmation code to your account email address
3. Enter the code in the dialog to start the download

Each code is single-use and expires after the period shown in the dialog. Use **Resend** in the dialog to receive a new code. This step protects your exports if someone gains access to a logged-in session: downloading the archive also requires access to your inbox.

## Organization Exports

The **Organization exports** card lists each organization you may export. An organization export covers work members did in the organization's context, plus the data the organization owns outright. It does not include members' personal activity.

Selection is by organization, not by membership: a former member's organization work is included, and a current member's personal work is not.

Only one export per organization runs at a time — two admins requesting an export share one job rather than generating duplicate copies.

The export history shows a badge on each row indicating whether it is a personal or an organization export, and each export file's header records which kind of export it contains.
