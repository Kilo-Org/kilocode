# Managing Your Team

Every person on the team has one of four roles: _Owner_, _Admin_, _Billing Manager_, or _Member_.

- **Owners** have full administrative oversight including billing, seat allocation, and model/provider selection. Only Owners can grant the Owner role or change or remove an existing Owner's membership.
- **Admins** can do everything Owners can do — organization settings, billing, custom modes, SSO, and member management — except manage Owners.
- **Billing Managers** can manage billing and invite members.
- **Members** can use the Kilo Code extension and see data on the team's usage in the [usage dashboard](/docs/collaborate/teams/analytics).

Owners and Admins can conduct team management activities.

## Adding Team Members

1. **Navigate to Organization Tab** in your profile page and click on the team you want to manage
2. **Click "Invite Member"** button
3. **Enter the team member's email address**
4. **Select initial role** (Owner, Admin, Billing Manager, or Member)
5. Click **Send Invitation**

## Removing Team Members

When team members leave:

1. **Navigate to Organization tab**
2. **Find the departing member**
3. **Click "Remove" button**
4. **Confirm removal**
5. **Seat becomes available** immediately

## Changing Team Member Roles

Promote or demote team members as needed:

1. **Locate team member** in Organization tab
2. **Click role dropdown** next to their name
3. **Select new role** (Owner, Admin, Billing Manager, or Member)
4. **Confirm change**
5. **Member receives email notification**

### Viewing Team Status

The Organization tab shows:

- **Active members** with last activity
- **Pending invitations** awaiting acceptance
- **Role distribution** across the team

## Managing Sub-organizations

Organizations can contain direct **sub-organizations** — child organizations with their own members, credits, and policies. Owners and Admins can create sub-organizations from the [Organization dashboard](https://app.kilo.ai); Billing Managers and Members cannot. Hierarchies are one level deep: a sub-organization cannot itself have sub-organizations.

Once your organization has child organizations, a **Sub-organizations** entry appears in the organization sidebar. Each sub-organization gets its own set of pages:

- **Overview** — summary and management actions
- **People** — search and filter the sub-organization's members, roles, and pending invitations
- **Usage** — the sub-organization's AI usage
- **Credits** — credit balance and Kilo Pass reporting for the sub-organization
- **Distribute funds** — allocate part of the parent organization's pooled capacity to the sub-organization
- **Models** — model-policy visibility for the sub-organization
- **Permissions** — SSO and permission reporting

Sub-organizations you have access to also appear in the organization switcher, so you can switch into a sub-organization's context directly.
